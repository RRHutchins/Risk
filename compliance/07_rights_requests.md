# Individual rights requests

People can ask to see, correct, delete, restrict, object to or move their personal data.
Requests can be made in any form (email, phone, letter) and do not need to mention the law.

## Procedure

1. **Log it** below on the day it arrives. The deadline is **one calendar month**
   (extendable by two more months for complex requests — tell the person within the first
   month).
2. **Confirm identity** if you are unsure who is asking. Ask only for what you need.
3. **Work out who is the controller.**
   - Data in client documents: forward the request to the client within [5] working days
     and help them answer it. Do not reply on the client's behalf unless they tell you to.
   - Our own business records: we answer.
4. **Search** email, client folders, `outputs/` (run folders and `uploads`), reports and
   accounting records.
5. **Respond** in writing. For access requests, give a copy of the data plus the
   information in our privacy notice. Normally free of charge.
6. **Erasure:** delete the files (and the run folders in `outputs/`) unless we must keep
   them by law (e.g. invoices).
