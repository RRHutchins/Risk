# Personal data breach procedure and log

A breach is any loss, theft, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction
of personal data — for example a lost laptop, a report emailed to the wrong person, or a
hacked account.

## What to do

1. **Contain it straight away.** Recall the email, change passwords, revoke the API key in
   the Anthropic Console, remote-wipe the device.
2. **Assess the risk to the people affected.** What data, how many people, how sensitive,
   could it cause them harm (fraud, distress, loss)?
3. **If we are processing for a client:** tell the client within [24] hours. The client
   decides whether to report to the ICO.
4. **If we are the controller and there is a risk to people:** report to the ICO **within
   72 hours** of becoming aware — ico.org.uk/for-organisations/report-a-breach, or
   0303 123 1113. You can report in stages if you don't have all the facts yet.
5. **If there is a high risk to people:** tell them directly, without undue delay.
6. **Record every breach below**, even ones you do not report, with the reasons.
7. **Fix the cause** and update `03_retention_and_security.md`.
