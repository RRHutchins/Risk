# Data processing terms (schedule to client engagement)

*Attach to your engagement letter or terms of business for each client. Article 28 UK GDPR
requires these terms whenever we process personal data on a client's behalf. Have them
checked before first use.*

**Between:** [Client name] (the "Client", controller) and [Business name], ICO registration
ZC288139 (the "Supplier", processor).

1. **Subject matter and duration.** The Supplier reviews documents supplied by the Client to
   identify risks and prepare a report, for the duration of the engagement.
2. **Nature and purpose.** Reading, analysing (including with an AI service), storing and
   deleting document content to produce the report.
3. **Personal data and data subjects.** Names, job titles, signatures, contact details and
   CVs of people named in the documents. The Client will not knowingly send special
   category or criminal offence data and will remove unnecessary personal data (for example
   CVs) where it can.
4. **Instructions.** The Supplier processes personal data only on the Client's documented
   instructions (this engagement), unless the law requires otherwise, and will tell the
   Client if it thinks an instruction breaks data protection law.
5. **Confidentiality.** Everyone authorised to process the data is bound by confidentiality.
6. **Security.** The Supplier keeps appropriate technical and organisational measures,
   including encrypted storage and restricted access.
7. **Sub-processors.** The Client authorises the Supplier to use Anthropic, PBC (USA) to
   provide the AI service, under a written contract with equivalent data protection terms.
   The Supplier will give [14] days' notice of any new sub-processor; the Client may object.
8. **International transfers.** Data is transferred to the USA for the AI service, protected
   by [the UK Extension to the Data Privacy Framework / the UK IDTA or Addendum].
9. **Data subject rights.** The Supplier will help the Client respond to requests from
   individuals and pass any request it receives to the Client within [5] working days.
10. **Assistance.** The Supplier will help the Client with security, breach notification and
    DPIAs, taking into account the nature of the processing.
11. **Breaches.** The Supplier will notify the Client without undue delay, and within [24]
    hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Client's data.
12. **Deletion.** The Supplier deletes the documents and working files within [90] days of
    delivering the report (or sooner on request), keeping only the final report for
    [6 years] as a professional record, unless the Client asks for it to be deleted.
13. **Audit.** The Supplier will provide information needed to show compliance with these
    terms and allow reasonable audits on [30] days' notice.
