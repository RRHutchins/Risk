Site Information

1 Site description
The site is a former industrial yard. A site investigation was carried out in 2019 with three
boreholes. Made ground up to 3 m deep was found.

2 Existing buildings
Asbestos may be present in the existing pavilion. No asbestos survey has been carried out.

3 Access
Access is from Mill Lane only, which is shared with a primary school. No deliveries between
08:15-09:00 and 15:00-15:45 on school days.
