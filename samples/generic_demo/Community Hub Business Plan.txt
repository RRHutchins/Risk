Community Hub Business Plan (fictional demonstration)

1 Objectives
The Hub will open a community cafe and training kitchen by September 2027 and cover its running costs from year two.

2 Funding
The project will be funded entirely by the Lottery grant of GBP 400,000, with a decision expected in March 2027.

3 Staffing
The training kitchen will be run by the Kitchen Manager, who is the only member of staff with the required food hygiene qualification.

4 Premises
The lease of the former library has not yet been signed. The landlord may require the Hub to indemnify it against all losses arising from the use of the building.

5 Partners
Local food businesses may donate surplus stock to the cafe, which would reduce food costs.
